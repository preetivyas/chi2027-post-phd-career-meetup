You've Got a PhD. Now What?

Navigating Career Crossroads in HCI

About This Meetup

The transition from graduate study to professional careers involves multiple crossroads, from deciding between academic and non-academic paths to navigating an evolving job market. While CHI offers opportunities for mentorship, networking, and career development across different stages of graduate education and early careers, there remains a need for spaces where researchers can openly exchange experiences and strategies for navigating these transitions.

This meet-up brings together PhD students, recent graduates, postdoctoral researchers, and senior researchers to share experiences, resources, advice, and support. Through informal networking, participant-driven breakout discussions, and a collective reflection activity, attendees will explore career crossroads together and contribute to a shared resource for the broader CHI community.
